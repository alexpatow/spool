---
title: Credits
description: Projects that shaped Spool's design.
---

- [Splink](https://github.com/moj-analytical-services/splink) showed how to use SQL in DuckDB to find and compare candidate pairs.
- [eve](https://eve.dev/docs/reference/agent-files) inspired Spool's project layout. A directory of files defines a project and can be reviewed in Git.
- [nomenklatura](https://github.com/opensanctions/nomenklatura) and [OpenSanctions](https://www.opensanctions.org/) informed how Spool keeps evidence tied to its source and records positive, negative and uncertain judgements.
- [MusicBrainz](https://musicbrainz.org/) and [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/) provided the model for keeping old IDs usable after a merge.
- [Senzing](https://senzing.com/) inspired explanations of why two records matched or stayed separate.
- [GoldenMatch](https://github.com/benseverndev-oss/goldenmatch) informed the separation of matching from identity changes and the practice of recording which configuration produced a decision.
- [matchlab](https://github.com/uktrade/matchlab) informed the use of content hashes in record IDs.
- [dedupe](https://github.com/dedupeio/dedupe) and [pyJedAI](https://github.com/AI-team-UoA/pyJedAI) showed how to choose a clustering method independently of how pairs are scored.
