Say why two records share an identity, or what holds one together

Usage: spool explain [OPTIONS] <RECORD> [RECORD]

Arguments:
  <RECORD>  A record; alone, an identity, by its ID or by any of its records
  [RECORD]  A second record, to say why the two share an identity or do not

Options:
  -h, --help  Print help (see more with '--help')

Output options:
  -o, --output <OUTPUT>  Output format [default: table] [possible values: table, json, ndjson, csv]
      --json             Shorthand for `--output json`

Server options:
      --url <URL>      The server [env: SPOOL_URL] [default: http://127.0.0.1:7765]
      --token <TOKEN>  A token for that server; `spool dev` needs none [env: SPOOL_TOKEN]

Global options:
  -q, --quiet  Print only the result and errors: no hints or progress

Examples:
  spool explain crm:c-1 crm:c-2
  spool explain crm:c-1
