A company is a business the text names: a legal entity such as a corporation,
a limited company or a partnership.

Quote the name exactly as the text writes it, with its legal form if the text
gives one (Ltd, Inc, Corp, GmbH, AG, SA), and without the article or any other
word around it. Every place the text names a company is a mention of its own,
however often the same company is named.

A product, a person, a place or a public body is not a company.

Attributes:

- `name`: the name as quoted.
